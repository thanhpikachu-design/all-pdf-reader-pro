Privacy Policy — All PDF Reader: Scan & Sign

Package ID: com.thanhdesign.pdfconverterpro  |  Developer: Thanh Design Studio  |  Effective Date: November 27, 2026

All PDF Reader: Scan & Sign ("the App") is developed by Thanh Design Studio as a privacy-first document reader, scanner, converter, and PDF editor for Android. All document reading, electronic signing (eSign), merging, splitting, password locking/unlocking, watermarking, Office viewing (DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, TXT), and AI OCR text recognition run 100% locally on your device. We never upload, transmit, store, or inspect any of your documents, signatures, passwords, or scanned images on any external server.

1. Information We Collect & Share

  • Personal Files & Document Contents: Zero collected, zero shared. Every PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, scanned image, signature, and password stays strictly on your phone or tablet.
  • Anonymous App Diagnostics, Performance & Usage Analytics (Google Firebase): To keep the App crash-free, fast, and reliable, we use no-cost Google Firebase services (Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics 4, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring, Firebase Remote Config, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Firebase In-App Messaging). These services automatically process non-personal technical signals encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS:
    • Crash logs & ANR stack traces (to diagnose and fix software bugs);
    • Performance diagnostics (cold-start duration and PDF tool latency);
    • Aggregated app interactions (screen views, general feature usage counts, country/language, device brand/model, Android OS version, and app version — never document names or contents);
    • Firebase Installation ID & FCM Notification Token (solely to deliver configuration defaults, contextual in-app tips, and optional push notifications).
  • Advertising ID Disabled: Because advertising is disabled, collection of the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and ad personalization signals is explicitly turned OFF.
  • No Sale or Third-Party Data Sharing: We never sell, rent, or share user data with third-party data brokers or advertisers. Google LLC processes diagnostic/analytics telemetry strictly as an infrastructure service provider under the Firebase Privacy & Security terms.
  • Contacts, SMS, Precise Location & Passwords: Never requested and never collected.

2. How Device Permissions Are Used Locally

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) & Storage/Media Permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Requested strictly after displaying a clear in-app Prominent Disclosure dialog. Because PDF contracts, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, and PowerPoint presentations reside across diverse folders (Download, Documents, email/chat attachment folders, SD cards), this permission enables the App's core document manager to list, search, open, annotate, sign, merge, split, and save edited documents directly in your chosen device folders (Documents/AllPdfPro and Pictures/AllPdfPro). Users may also skip full storage access and open individual files via the Android system file picker (SAF) or Android Photo Picker at any time.
  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely when you open the Scan to PDF tool to photograph physical paper documents, receipts, or ID cards for on-device perspective cropping and PDF conversion.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used strictly when you explicitly enter a webpage URL inside the Web to PDF converter tool so your device's built-in Android WebView can load that webpage and print it to a local PDF file. In future updates, if optional advertising (such as Google AdMob) is enabled, standard Google Play Services advertising identifiers will be governed by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) with full user consent controls. In the current release, third-party ad SDKs are disabled.

3. Data Retention, Security & Deletion Control

Because we maintain no external servers or user databases, all app preferences (such as selected language, theme mode, favorite bookmarks, and recent reading progress) are stored strictly in your Android device's private sandboxed storage. Uninstalling the App or clearing App Data in Android Settings permanently deletes all local preferences and history immediately. Output PDFs and images saved in Documents/AllPdfPro remain under your full ownership and can be deleted anytime using the App or any file manager.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose productivity and office document utility intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
